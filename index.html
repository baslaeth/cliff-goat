How payouts and odds are calculated

Money uses integer cents. Payouts use the published two-decimal multiplier, rounded to the nearest cent, with half cents rounded up. To keep conditional odds strictly decreasing, the smallest upward cent correction is applied if needed. Only Easy stakes of $1.11, $1.19, $1.24, $1.32, $1.44, $1.45, and $1.57 need this: one extra cent at step 6. The displayed dollar payout is exactly what Collect pays.

For stake S cents and settled payout Aᵢ cents: p₁ = 97S / (100A₁); pᵢ = Aᵢ₋₁ / Aᵢ thereafter. The factors cancel, so survival to step i is 97S / (100Aᵢ). The 3% edge is applied once per played round. Cancellation is separate. Outcome sampling uses full integer fractions and unbiased cryptographic rejection sampling; displayed percentage rounding is never used for decisions.
